# Product Context - Fleet Management System

## Why This Project Exists
Malaysian government institutions need efficient fleet management to optimize vehicle utilization, reduce operational costs, and ensure compliance with government standards. Current manual processes are inefficient and lack proper tracking.

## Problems It Solves

### Current Pain Points
1. **Manual Booking Process**: Paper-based or email booking requests causing delays
2. **Poor Vehicle Utilization**: No visibility into vehicle availability and usage patterns
3. **Maintenance Oversight**: Missed maintenance schedules leading to breakdowns
4. **Fuel Cost Control**: No tracking of fuel card usage and expenses
5. **KRJ Management**: Difficulty managing official vehicles assigned to positions
6. **Compliance Issues**: Manual processes don't meet KRISA standards
7. **Reporting Gaps**: No centralized reporting for fleet analytics

### Business Impact
- **Operational Inefficiency**: Time wasted on manual coordination
- **Cost Overruns**: Poor fuel management and maintenance planning
- **Compliance Risk**: Non-adherence to government IT standards
- **Resource Waste**: Underutilized vehicles and over-booking conflicts

## How It Should Work

### User Experience Goals

#### For Fleet Managers
- **Dashboard Overview**: Real-time fleet status and key metrics
- **Easy Booking Management**: Quick approval/rejection of requests
- **Maintenance Alerts**: Proactive notifications for service due
- **Cost Tracking**: Clear visibility into fuel and maintenance costs

#### For Regular Users
- **Simple Booking**: Intuitive vehicle booking with calendar view
- **Status Transparency**: Clear booking status and approval workflow
- **Self-Service**: Minimal admin intervention for standard requests

#### For Drivers
- **Schedule Clarity**: Clear daily assignments and routes
- **Mobile Access**: Easy schedule viewing on mobile devices
- **Update Capability**: Simple status updates and mileage reporting

#### For Administrators
- **Complete Control**: Full system configuration and user management
- **Comprehensive Reports**: Detailed analytics and compliance reports
- **Data Management**: Easy import/export and backup capabilities

### Core Workflows

#### Vehicle Booking Flow
1. User selects vehicle type and dates
2. System checks availability and conflicts
3. User fills booking details (purpose, destination, passengers)
4. System routes to appropriate approver
5. Approval/rejection with notifications
6. Calendar integration and driver assignment

#### Maintenance Management Flow
1. System alerts when maintenance due
2. Maintenance staff schedules service
3. Vehicle marked unavailable during service
4. Service completion updates with costs
5. Next maintenance date calculated
6. Vehicle returned to available pool

#### KRJ Assignment Flow
1. Position-based vehicle allocation
2. Usage tracking and mileage recording
3. Transfer procedures when positions change
4. Priority booking for official duties
