# Fleet Management System - Project Brief

## Project Overview
**Fleet Management System** for Malaysian government/institutional use, built with Laravel Filament to manage vehicle bookings, maintenance, fuel cards, and administrative functions.

## Core Requirements
- **3 Main Modules**: Vehicle Management, User Management, Administration
- **Polymorphic Booking System**: Scalable architecture for vehicles, facilities, rooms
- **Malaysian Context**: KRJ (Official Vehicles), Kad Minyak (Fuel Cards), government compliance
- **Technology**: Laravel 11 + Filament 3.x + MySQL 8.0
- **Compliance**: KRISA guidelines by JDN

## Key Business Goals
1. **Automate** vehicle booking and allocation processes
2. **Track** vehicle maintenance and service history  
3. **Manage** fuel card allocation and usage (Kad Minyak)
4. **Oversee** official vehicle (KRJ) assignments
5. **Provide** centralized fleet administration

## Success Criteria
- Improved operational efficiency
- Better resource utilization
- Enhanced tracking and reporting
- Streamlined approval processes
- Cost management and control
- KRISA compliance achievement

## Project Scope
**In Scope**:
- Vehicle booking with 10 purpose types
- Maintenance scheduling and tracking
- Fuel card management
- KRJ (official vehicle) management
- User management with role-based access
- Calendar integration
- Approval workflows
- Comprehensive reporting

**Future Scope**:
- Facility and room booking (polymorphic expansion)
- Mobile application
- Advanced analytics and ML

## Stakeholders
- **Fleet Administrators**: Full system control
- **Fleet Managers**: Vehicle allocation oversight
- **Drivers**: Schedule viewing and updates
- **Regular Users**: Booking requests
- **Maintenance Staff**: Service record management
